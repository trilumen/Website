Warranty Coverage

Complimentary 3-Year Parts Warranty

All TriLumen Systems installations come standard with the following minimum coverage:

  • Parts Coverage: Full replacement coverage for all LED nodes, aluminum channel, and power supplies for 3 years, provided by the manufacturer.
  • Labor Coverage: 90 days coverage against defects in installation workmanship.
  • Note: Service calls for parts failure after 90 days are billable unless the TriLumen Assurance Plan is purchased.

The TriLumen Assurance Plan (5-Year Total)

Extend your peace of mind and minimize service costs with our comprehensive premium plan.

  • In-House Guarantee: Direct coverage from TriLumen Systems—no third parties, no technicalities.
  • Zero Out-of-Pocket: All labor and part replacement costs are covered for the full 5 years. You will never pay a service fee for a covered component. Ever.
  • Extended Duration: Extends full coverage for all hardware and labor for a total of 5 years from installation date.
