What We Do

Six disciplines, drawn from national retail, manufacturing, and government work, and fitted to businesses of any size.

BIReporting

BI Reporting & Dashboards

We build the dashboards and scorecards leadership trusts to run the business, and the data pipeline beneath them, so the numbers are fast, accurate, and inexpensive to maintain.

  • Dashboard design & build (Power BI, Tableau)
  • KPI scorecard & reporting design
  • Data warehouse & pipeline architecture (Google BigQuery, managed PostgreSQL, DuckDB + dbt)
  • Financial modeling & Excel-based reporting
  • Data source integration
AIAutomation

Copilot & AI Automation

We deploy Microsoft Copilot and design AI-assisted workflows that remove manual, repetitive work, built with your team and not around them.

  • Microsoft Copilot deployment across business functions
  • AI-assisted workflow design
  • Automation strategy & rollout
ClCloud

SharePoint & Cloud Migrations

We build company-wide intranets and manage cloud migrations designed for adoption, not just a completed deployment checklist.

  • SharePoint intranet builds
  • Cloud environment migration
  • Systems integration
ChChange

Change Management & Adoption

The best system in the world fails if nobody uses it. We build the training and rollout plan that makes new systems stick.

  • Training & documentation
  • Rollout planning
  • Adoption tracking
ErERP

ERP & WMS Support

Guidance and hands-on support for ERP, warehouse management, delivery, and POS platforms, across whatever system you already run.

  • ERP implementation & migration support
  • Warehouse management system (WMS) migration
  • Delivery & logistics platform integration
  • POS deployment & retail systems integration (Square, Clover, Toast, or your existing system)
AuWorkflow

Workflow Automation

Power Automate-driven automation that removes repetitive manual work and frees your team for higher-value work.

  • Process automation builds
  • Manual task elimination
  • Cross-system workflow design
