What We Do

Six areas of work, built from real experience at national retail, manufacturing, and government scale — applied to businesses of any size.

BIReporting

BI Reporting & Dashboards

We build the dashboards and scorecards leadership actually trusts to run the business, and the data pipeline underneath them, so the numbers are fast, accurate, and don't cost a fortune to maintain.

  • Dashboard design & build (Power BI, Tableau)
  • KPI scorecard & reporting design
  • Data warehouse & pipeline architecture (Google BigQuery, managed PostgreSQL, DuckDB + dbt)
  • Financial modeling & Excel-based reporting
  • Data source integration
AIAutomation

Copilot & AI Automation

We deploy Microsoft Copilot and design AI-assisted workflows that remove manual, repetitive work — built with your team, not around them.

  • AI-assisted workflow design
  • Automation strategy & rollout
ClCloud

SharePoint & Cloud Migrations

We build company-wide intranets and manage cloud migrations designed for real adoption, not just a completed deployment checklist.

  • Systems integration
ChChange

Change Management & Adoption

The best system in the world fails if nobody uses it. We build the training and rollout plan that makes new systems actually stick.

  • Training & documentation
  • Rollout planning
  • Adoption tracking

Guidance and hands-on support for ERP, warehouse management, and delivery platforms — we work with whatever system you already run.
