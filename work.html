How these get built

Worked example: Brightwell Mercantile, Square to QuickBooks to Excel.

1

Inputs

A Square sales export, the QuickBooks deposits, and the Excel master someone keeps by hand. We map all three before building anything.

2

Logic

Match each deposit to its Square batch, flag duplicate SKUs and customers, and re-run overnight. Anything that does not match becomes an exception.

3

The board

One exception queue, highest severity first, beside the job log. Training is included so it does not become another ignored tab.
