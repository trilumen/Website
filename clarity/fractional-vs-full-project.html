Fractional vs. Full Project: How to Know Which One Your Business Actually Needs

September 22, 2026

Not every systems problem needs a full-scale project with a formal assessment and a multi-week timeline. Some just need a few hours from someone who knows what they're looking at.

The size of the fix, not the size of the business

It's a common assumption that a small business needs small-scale help and a large company needs a full project. In practice, the right fit depends on the size of the actual problem, not the size of the company. A large company with one broken automation might need exactly the same few hours of help as a five-person shop.

Signs a retainer or a quick fix is the right fit

If the issue is contained, one broken automation, one dashboard that needs building, one integration that's out of sync, a fixed-scope engagement or a small monthly retainer usually solves it without needing a broader review of everything else in your systems.

Signs you actually need a full project

If the problem touches multiple systems at once, or if fixing one thing keeps surfacing three more things that also need attention, that's usually a sign the underlying architecture needs a real look, not just a patch. That's when a proper assessment earns its cost, it prevents a series of small fixes from turning into a series of small disappointments.

You don't have to know which one before you call

Most people aren't sure which category their problem falls into, and that's fine. Sorting that out is usually the first five minutes of a real conversation, not something you need to diagnose yourself before reaching out.

If you're not sure which one fits, that's a completely normal place to start from. A quick conversation usually sorts it out fast.
