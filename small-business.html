The Rules of the Road

48-Hour Response SLA

Routine maintenance and bug fixes are triaged, resolved, and safely deployed within 48 business hours. No fire drills, no random 2am texts, a schedule you can actually count on.

We Don't Let Hours Quietly Expire

If your systems are stable and you have hours left late in the billing cycle, we don't let them disappear. We proactively run a system tune-up, clearing logs and optimizing performance, and send you a full technical receipt of exactly what we did.

The Overage Guarantee

We never perform unbilled hours and never charge you retroactively. If a fix needs more time than your retainer holds, we pause, map out what's needed, and let you choose how to proceed.

Clear Scope, No Surprises

Retainers cover maintaining and troubleshooting the systems we build with you, and any others you're already running. A brand-new integration or feature is a separate, fixed-price project, we'll always tell you which one you're looking at.
