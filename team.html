Built Around Specialists, Not Generalists

The right expert for the actual problem in front of us, not one person stretched across every discipline.

We don't put headshots and bios on this page. Not because there's nothing behind the work, but because the right team for your project depends entirely on what your project actually needs.

Every engagement is led by a principal consultant from first call through delivery, backed by a network of vetted specialists brought in based on the specific problem at hand. You're not getting a generalist stretched thin across a dozen disciplines. You're getting the right specialist for the right part of the job, the same reason we don't name clients on this site either. The work speaks for itself, and so does the structure behind it.

One consistent point of contact leads every project. The specialists change depending on what you actually need, the relationship and accountability don't.

Leadership

Solutions Architect (Principal Consultant)

I lead every engagement personally, from the first call through final delivery. I design the overall system architecture, decide which specialists a given project actually needs, and stay the throughline from strategy to execution myself, so nothing gets lost in a handoff to someone you've never spoken to.

Data & Analytics

Analytics Engineers (dbt / SQL Experts)

Build the transformation logic that turns raw data into something trustworthy, the SQL and dbt models underneath every dashboard and report we deliver.

Database Administrators / Cloud Engineers

Handle the infrastructure a system actually runs on: database performance, cloud architecture, and making sure the foundation can handle real production load, not just a demo.

Business Intelligence (BI) Developers

Build the dashboards and reporting interfaces themselves, translating a data model into something a team actually opens and trusts every day.

Systems & Integration

Microsoft Power Platform Architects

Design and build on Power Apps, Power Automate, and Power BI specifically, the backbone of most of our workflow automation and internal tool work.

Integration Developers (iPaaS Experts)

Connect the systems that weren't built to talk to each other, a CRM to an ERP, one platform to another, handling the sync logic, error handling, and edge cases that make an integration actually reliable.

ERP / WMS Functional Consultants

Bring deep, hands-on experience with specific ERP and warehouse management platforms, understanding not just the software but how a real warehouse or finance team actually uses it day to day.

Strategy & Adoption

Business Systems Analysts (BSAs)

Translate between what a business actually needs and what gets built, mapping current-state processes and making sure a technical solution solves the real problem, not just the one that got written down first.

Change Management & Training Specialists

Handle the adoption side of every engagement: training, rollout planning, and the ongoing support that makes sure a new system actually gets used, not just installed.
