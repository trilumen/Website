Quick Questions

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes, always. The domain is registered in your name, on your own account. We manage the DNS for you while you're with us, but it's never ours to hold.

Can I just get the website, without POS?

The launch packages are bundled on purpose, since that's genuinely what opening a business needs. If you only need POS, see our POS setup and install options instead.

What if I'm outside the NYC-to-Philadelphia area?

Everything in a launch package can be done remotely. On-site POS installation is swapped for our remote setup, done over a video call.

What happens after the first free month of Site Care?

It continues at the standard monthly rate unless you tell us to cancel, so your site stays updated, backed up, and secure without you having to think about it.
